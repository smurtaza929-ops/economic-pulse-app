About Economic Pulse

Economic Pulse is a web application designed to help users understand current economic conditions through key economic indicators.

Economic Indicators

  • Inflation
  • Unemployment
  • Real GDP
  • Federal Funds (Interest) Rate
    •

    These indicators are analyzed to generate an Economic Health Score and overall economic assessment.

  • Moderate
